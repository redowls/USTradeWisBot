- **Expected impact:** Eliminates same-symbol double-entries from fill latency. On today's data this alone removes the −$87.36 duplicate leg → day swings from −$35.61 toward roughly breakeven/positive.

## IMP-002 — 2026-06-18

- **Problem:** Positions opened 06-16 (C/AMZN/BAC) were held for **TWO overnights** (06-16→06-18) — a repeated breach of the no-overnight invariant, flagged by pre-market research three days running. Alpaca order history: at 06-16 15:55 ET **no sell orders fired at all**; at 06-17 15:55 the flatten ran chaotically (burst of ~10 duplicate AMZN market-sells, most auto-canceled, one filled; C/BAC stops canceled but positions NOT liquidated); only 06-18 closed C/BAC. - **Root cause:** `exits.flatten_all` called fire-and-forget bulk `broker.close_all_positions(cancel_orders=True)`, which (a) races the async order-cancel so `held_for_orders` blocks the liquidation, (b) returns a 207 multi-status the SDK does not raise on (partial failure invisible), and (c) `engine.tick` then set `self.flattened_on = now.date()` **unconditionally** after `eod_flatten()` returned — so a flatten that left positions open was never retried, and the day was declared flat.
- **Change:** `bot/broker.py` — new `close_position(symbol)` (per-symbol DELETE /v2/positions/{symbol}). `bot/exits.py` — `flatten_all` now cancels working orders FIRST, then closes each position individually (so a still-working bracket leg can't block its own liquidation). `bot/engine.py` — `eod_flatten()` returns bool: it re-queries `open_position_symbols()` after liquidation, marks a logbook trade CLOSED **only** once its broker position is confirmed gone, leaves any unconfirmed position OPEN + fires `error_alert`, and returns False; `tick()` sets `flattened_on` **only when `eod_flatten()` returns True** → an incomplete flatten retries next tick instead of stranding overnight. No risk limit altered (paper endpoint, MAX_RISK_PCT, DAILY_LOSS_HALT_PCT 8.0, MAX_CONCURRENT 3, no-overnight rules all untouched — this strengthens the no-overnight rule).
- **Validation:** New `tests/test_eod_flatten.py` (6 tests) replays the 06-16→06-18 scenario: cancel-before-close ordering, one position raising must not stop the rest, BAC-stays-open→retry/alert, C-confirmed-flat→closed at recorded 143.765, dry-run no-op, and tick-gating retry. Full suite **35 passed**; `scripts.smoke_test` ALL GREEN; `scripts.check_engine` ALL GREEN. Service restarted clean.
- **Expected impact:** Eliminates silent naked-overnight holds: any position that does not confirm flat at 15:55 keeps being re-liquidated each tick and raises an alert, instead of being declared flat and stranded. Directly prevents the repeat of the 06-16 two-night hold.

## IMP-008 — 2026-06-30

- **Problem:** On the best day of incubation (5W/1L, +$297.04, +3.71%), the one real defect was a **missed entry, not a loss**. AMD — the day's strongest mover (gapped >2% at the open, later ran to ~578) — was the bot's first entry attempt (09:30:22 ET) but Alpaca **rejected the entire bracket** with a 422: `take_profit.limit_price must be >= base_price + 0.01` (base_price **554.29**, code 42210000). The entry was silently lost — it never reached the `signals`/`trades` tables, only `bot.log`. First occurrence of this rejection class in incubation.
- **Root cause:** The plan's `entry_price`, `stop_price` and `take_profit_price` are all anchored to **`ev["close"]` — the signal-bar close** (~542 for AMD at the open) — but the order is a **MARKET buy that fills at the live price** (554.29). Because `take_profit = entry + stop_distance·RR_RATIO` and `stop_distance ≥ MIN_STOP_PCT` (1.5%), the TP sits at minimum `entry·1.0225`; AMD's >2% open gap pushed the live base_price **above** that TP, so the bracket's take-profit leg was below the market and Alpaca 422'd the whole order. ## IMP-039 — 2026-08-21

- **Problem:** **IMP-038 fired for the first time on its first live session, and it fired on a healthy trade.** At **12:04:22 ET** the bot logged `UNPROTECTED UNH: position open with no working bracket stop leg (live 390.61, plan stop 384.09)` and raised a Telegram alert. **UNH #275 was not unprotected — its stop leg was selling the position at that exact moment.** One minute later: `EXIT UNH STOP pl=$3.98 (0.1701%)`.
- **Measurement (Alpaca order timeline for parent `e71f017d`, read back through the `alpaca` MCP):** the stop had been trailed twice by IMP-013 (384.09 → 390.12 at 10:53:27 → **390.55** at 10:58:56), rotating the leg id to `c3e90c63`. Then:

  | event | broker timestamp |
  |---|---|
  | limit leg `52f81840` canceled | 2026-08-21T16:04:19.610486Z |
  | stop leg `c3e90c63` submitted | 2026-08-21T16:04:19.672162Z |

  | engine poll logs `UNPROTECTED UNH` | 2026-08-21T16:04:22Z |

  The poll landed **inside the fill second**. `resolve_stop_leg` returned None because **FILLED is not a PATCHable status** (`_REPLACEABLE = {NEW, ACCEPTED, HELD, PENDING_NEW}`), while `GET /v2/positions` had not yet dropped UNH — so `naked_position_action(position_held=True, …)` was asked a question about a position that no longer existed. The DB agrees to six decimals: trade #275 `exit_time` is **12:04:22.502483**, the same instant as the alert.
- **Root cause — and the part that matters is not the false alarm.** `resolve_stop_leg` returns None for three broker states (filled / cancelled / unresolvable) and IMP-038 correctly separated "position gone" from "position naked" **but not "stop filled" from "stop cancelled"**. Its enforcement arm is gated on `live <= the trade's ORIGINAL plan stop` — and **every ordinary stop-out satisfies that by construction**, because a stop fills at or through its own price. **111 of 261 closed trades exited on STOP.** **MU #273 missed the identical window by 53 seconds the same morning:** stop leg `ccaa9487` filled **14:19:01.365898Z @ 961.31** against a **961.59** plan stop; the next engine poll was 14:19:54Z, by which time the position had cleared. Had that poll landed where UNH's did, IMP-038 would have cancelled the sibling leg and sent `DELETE /v2/positions/MU` against a book the broker had already flattened — **a second sell on a flat position, i.e. an unintended short**, which is precisely the outcome `_handle_naked_position`'s own docstring says cancelling-before-selling rules out. It rules out *both bracket legs* filling; it does not rule out *the bot* racing *its own filled stop*. **The hole was reachable, was reached once today on the harmless branch, and its dangerous branch is the majority exit path.**
- **Change:** **Exit-path INTEGRITY, strictly narrowing — NO entry condition, NO signal, NO scorer, NO sizing, NO stop distance, NO take-profit, NO trail/break-even trigger, NO risk limit, NO watchlist touched.** (1) `bot/exits.py`: `resolve_stop_leg` refactored onto a new private `_walk_stop_leg`, which follows the replace chain and returns the terminal leg **whatever its status**; `resolve_stop_leg` is now a thin status filter over it and its contract is byte-for-byte unchanged. New public `stop_leg_filled(entry_order, fetch, max_hops=5)` reports whether that terminal leg reached **FILLED or PARTIALLY_FILLED** (`_STOP_LEG_FILLED`). (2) `bot/engine.py`: the `stop_leg is None` branch now short-circuits on `exits.stop_leg_filled(...)` **before** the `/v2/positions` lookup — a filled stop is an exit in progress and belongs to `check_exits`, not to the naked-position path.
- **Why the guard is narrow, and why IMP-038 survives intact:** **CANCELED is untouched.** Alpaca's OCO *cancels* the stop leg on the take-profit path, so **all ten of IMP-038's measured naked windows (BAC #149's 26m34s included) are CANCELED, not FILLED** — they still alert, and still enforce at/below the plan stop. Only FILLED/PARTIALLY_FILLED is newly silent, and both are states in which the shares are sold or the same order is still working the remainder. `PARTIALLY_FILLED` is deliberately counted as protected for that second reason. An unresolvable leg (no leg, dead-end chain, hop limit) is **never** reported as filled — fail-safe in the direction of still alerting.
